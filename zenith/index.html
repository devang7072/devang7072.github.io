Zenith: Minimalist Launcher

com.zenithfocus.launcher 100% Local-First Zero Tracking Effective: Nov 2026

Privacy Commitment: Zenith: Minimalist Launcher is architected around a strict offline-first, local-only philosophy. We operate zero cloud databases, collect zero personal telemetry, and store 100% of your notes and settings exclusively on your device.

01 Application Overview & Philosophy

Zenith is a Scandinavian-inspired minimalist Android home screen launcher built to promote intentional digital wellbeing. By replacing addictive icons and notification badges with pure typography, Nordic color accents, and offline notes, Zenith helps you reclaim focus. This Privacy Policy outlines how your device and app data are handled.

02 Zero Personal Data Collection

Zenith does not collect, record, process, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). Specifically:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to sign in, create an account, or provide an email address, phone number, or name.
  • No Telemetry or Profiling: We do not include tracking or analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK, or Mixpanel). Your screen time, unlock counts, and app usage patterns are strictly computed in-memory on your device.
  • No Remote Servers: We operate no external databases or cloud sync APIs for user data.

03 Local-First Data Storage

All user-generated content and preferences remain securely sandboxed on your physical Android device using Google's modern Room (SQLite) database and Jetpack Preferences DataStore:

  • Quick Notes: Scratchpad notes and pinned homescreen notes are saved locally in private app storage.
  • Preferences & Customization: Your selected Scandinavian clock styles, Nordic OLED accent palettes, and pinned home favorites are stored in local DataStore files.
  • Hidden Apps Vault: The list of apps you choose to lock away is saved locally and never shared.

04 Android Device Permissions

Zenith requests only the essential Android permissions required to operate as a functional homescreen replacement:

Permission Technical Purpose Data Treatment
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Allows the launcher to query installed applications to populate your alphabetical App Drawer and homescreen shortcuts. Processed entirely in memory; your list of installed apps is never saved to disk, logged, or shared off-device.
USE_BIOMETRIC Enables optional fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN protection for your hidden apps vault. Managed directly by Android's hardware-backed TrustZone/TEE. Zenith never accesses, reads, or stores biometric raw data.
EXPAND_STATUS_BAR Allows you to swipe down on the home screen to open your notification drawer without reaching the top of tall devices. No user data accessed; simple system UI trigger.
VIBRATE Provides subtle tactile haptic feedback during search, long-press actions, and app drawer scrolling. No user data accessed.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional Android 13+ permission for mindful notifications or widget updates if enabled. Local system notifications only.

05 Third-Party Services & Advertising

Zenith does not force any banner ads, popups, or interstitial ads during your daily launcher usage. To sustain independent development, Zenith offers an optional, voluntary "Watch an Ad to Support" button located deep within the Preferences sheet:

  • Voluntary Rewarded Ads: Powered by the official Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. Ads are loaded and displayed only when you explicitly tap to watch one.
  • AdMob Privacy Compliance: Google AdMob may collect standard device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and crash logs to serve ads and prevent fraudulent clicks. For details on Google's privacy practices, visit Google Privacy & Terms.

06 Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Zenith is a general audience utility application designed for all ages. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe, distraction-free, and contains no age-inappropriate content.

07 Data Deletion & Retention

Because all data resides exclusively in your phone's internal storage sandbox, you retain complete sovereign control over your information:

  • Instant Deletion: Clearing Zenith's app storage in Android Settings → Apps → Zenith → Clear Storage permanently erases all notes, favorites, and settings.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application automatically and irreversibly removes 100% of all local database files.

08 Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect new Android OS features or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted to this URL with a revised effective date.
