Privacy Policy for PDF Merger

Effective Date: November 2026 • Version: 1.0 • Package: com.devang.pdf.merger.combine

100% On-Device Processing Guarantee: PDF Merger is engineered with a strict offline-first architecture. All PDF parsing, page extraction, merging, watermark stamping, and thumbnail generation are executed entirely on your local device using embedded Apache PDFBox. Your documents and personal files NEVER leave your phone, are NEVER uploaded to any cloud server, and are NEVER transmitted over the internet.

1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Merger: Combine & Watermark ("we", "our", or "the application"), developed by Devang Bhumbhaliya, handles and protects your data. We respect your privacy and believe that mobile document utilities should serve you with maximum efficiency while respecting your data sovereignty. We do not collect, sell, or rent your personal documents or sensitive information.

2. Zero Cloud Uploads (100% Local Processing)

PDF Merger runs fully offline for all core document processing tasks:

  • Local PDFBox Engine: All PDF document manipulation—including page subset extraction, sequential joining, metadata creation, custom watermarking, and raster preview rendering—is performed strictly within the Android app's local memory sandbox.
  • Zero Server Transmission: The application does not maintain or connect to any remote server or cloud backend to process your files. Your confidential contracts, legal agreements, receipts, bank statements, and private records remain 100% in your hands.

3. Information Collection & Storage

We do not collect or harvest Personally Identifiable Information (PII):

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or payment details to use the application.
  • No Document Content Logging: We do not log, inspect, analyze, or retain any text, images, or data contained inside your PDF documents.
  • Local History Database: The application maintains an optional local document history (such as saved file names, timestamps, page counts, and local file URIs) stored exclusively in an on-device SQLite database within the app's private sandbox. This data never leaves your device.

4. Android Permissions & Storage Access

PDF Merger adheres to modern Android security principles and requests only necessary system capabilities:

  • Storage Access Framework (SAF): When you pick PDFs to merge or choose where to export your merged PDF, the standard Android system file picker is invoked. The app only accesses the specific files you explicitly select and grant permission to read or write. We do NOT require broad legacy storage permissions (such as MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).
  • Internet & Network State: android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are used solely to load non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob and to verify network status for ad rendering. No document files or document metadata are ever transferred across these connections.
  • Notification Permission: android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS is requested on Android 13+ solely to inform you when a background PDF merge operation has finished, allowing one-tap access to open the completed file.

5. Document Security, Storage & User Control

You have complete control over all data stored on your device:

  • Temporary Buffer Cleanup: Imported documents staged in the app's cache directory can be cleared at any time directly through the "Storage & Cleanup" section in Settings.
  • History Deletion: You can delete individual merged documents or clear your entire merge history with one tap.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application completely erases all app-specific private cache files, local settings, and SQLite history records. Files exported to your device's public Documents or Download folders remain safely in your storage under your control.

6. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support free development and updates, PDF Merger displays ads served by Google AdMob.

  • Advertising Identifiers: Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), general device metrics, and approximate location to serve compliant, non-personalized advertisements in accordance with Google Play Developer Policy.
  • Strict Separation: Your documents, file names, page counts, and custom watermark text are NEVER shared with or accessible by advertising networks or SDKs.
  • For details on Google's advertising practices, please read the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

PDF Merger is a general utility productivity tool and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately to have it purged.

8. Security Safeguards

Because all file operations take place locally on your Android device, your files are protected by Android's multi-layered application sandboxing, Linux file permissions, and secure scoped storage mechanisms.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements, new features, or regulatory standards. Any changes will be published directly on this web page with an updated effective date.
