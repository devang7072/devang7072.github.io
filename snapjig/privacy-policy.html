Privacy Policy

Application: SnapJig: Photo Jigsaw Puzzle • Package: com.snapjig.studio
Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Last Updated: November 18, 2026
Local-First Gameplay • On-Device Photo Processing • Zero Personal Tracking

Executive Summary: SnapJig is an offline-first HD jigsaw puzzle studio developed by Devang Bhumbhaliya Babubhai. All gameplay progress (100 campaign levels, earned stars, coin balance, daily streaks, custom photo puzzles, and audio settings) is stored exclusively on your local device. We do not operate remote user databases, do not require user accounts or logins, and never harvest, track, or sell your personal data. Custom photo puzzles created with your device camera or selected from your gallery are cropped, processed, and stored 100% on your device—your photos are never uploaded to any remote server or third party. Advertising is transparently delivered via Google AdMob in full compliance with the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how SnapJig: Photo Jigsaw Puzzle ("the Application", "the Game", "we", "us", or "our"), engineered by Devang Bhumbhaliya Babubhai ("Developer"), distributed under package identifier com.snapjig.studio, handles user information.

We believe that player privacy, creative freedom, and enjoyable tactile gameplay go hand-in-hand. This policy informs players, parents, and legal guardians about our data practices across mobile and web editions of SnapJig.

2. Local-First Architecture & Data We Do NOT Collect

SnapJig is engineered with a strict sovereign local-first privacy architecture:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to register, provide an email address, or connect social media profiles to enjoy the complete game and its 100 campaign levels.
  • No Identity Harvesting: We do not collect names, phone numbers, contact lists, GPS locations, biometric identifiers, or hardware serial numbers.
  • Local State Persistence: All game data—including level completions, earned stars, virtual coins, hint balances, daily mission streaks, sound preferences, and downloaded offline puzzle packs—is stored directly in your device's private storage (Local Storage & IndexedDB).
  • No Server Analytics: We do not operate telemetry servers that log your puzzle completion times, hint usage, or in-game choices.

3. Camera Access & Custom Photo Puzzles

SnapJig features an interactive "Create Photo Puzzle" studio that allows players to turn their own photos into jigsaw puzzles with piece counts ranging from 4 up to 400 pieces:

  • Strictly On-Demand: SnapJig only requests camera or photo picker access when you explicitly tap the "Create" button and choose to take a new photo or select an existing picture from your device's photo gallery.
  • 100% On-Device Processing: Photos captured via the camera or chosen from your gallery are cropped, sliced into interlocking puzzle pieces, and stored entirely within your local device memory and local database.
  • Zero Remote Uploads: Your personal photos are NEVER transmitted, uploaded, streamed, or synchronized to any external server, cloud storage, AI training pipeline, or third-party service. You retain full ownership and privacy over every photo you convert into a puzzle.
  • Instant Deletion: You can delete any custom photo puzzle you have created at any time directly inside the in-game puzzle gallery with one tap.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

SnapJig is free to play and supported by transparent, non-intrusive mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC):

  • Ad Formats: AdMob may display non-obtrusive banner ads, occasional between-level interstitial ads, and optional rewarded video ads (allowing players to voluntarily watch an ad in exchange for bonus virtual coins).
  • Data Handled by Google AdMob: When ad requests are made, Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AAID), general non-precise IP-based location data, ad interaction metrics, and diagnostic information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Personalized Ads Toggle: You have full control over advertising personalization. Inside SnapJig's in-game Settings, you can easily toggle "Personalized Ads" on or off to request only contextual, non-personalized advertising.
  • Family & COPPA Standards: All ads are filtered to meet strict family-safe standards, ensuring no age-inappropriate content is ever displayed.

5. Android Operating System Permissions

SnapJig requests only the minimum essential operating system permissions required for core features:

  • android.permission.CAMERA: Used strictly to allow players to capture photos inside the "Create Photo Puzzle" studio. Camera hardware is never accessed in the background.
  • android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES (Android 13+) / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below): Used solely to permit players to choose an image from their gallery for custom puzzle generation.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required exclusively to load Google AdMob advertisements and stream optional web puzzle artwork previews.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

SnapJig includes a dedicated "Kids" collection (4 to 25 pieces) crafted for young puzzle enthusiasts and families. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect, harvest, or request personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Children can freely enjoy all 100 levels and offline features without providing any personal data.
  • If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately for immediate investigation and verification.

7. Data Security & Data Deletion

Because all gameplay data, custom puzzles, and settings reside entirely on your local device, you possess total authority over your data at all times:

  • In-App Clear Data: You can remove individual custom photo puzzles or reset level progress directly from the game interface.
  • Android System Clear Data: You can permanently wipe all locally saved data at any time via Android Settings: Settings > Apps > SnapJig > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Application Uninstall: Uninstalling SnapJig completely removes all locally stored game files and database entries from your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect application updates, new puzzle features, or legal and regulatory standards. Any changes will be published immediately on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of SnapJig following any update indicates your acceptance of the revised policy.
