Privacy Policy

VedaPrashna: The Living Gita · Effective Date: November 2026

Sovereign Privacy Commitment: VedaPrashna is built with an offline-first architecture. Your daily Japa Mala counts, reading streaks, and verse reflections reside locally on your device. We do not maintain user databases, sell your inquiries, or profile your spiritual habits.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how VedaPrashna: The Living Gita ("we", "our", or "the application"), developed by Devang Bhumbhaliya, collects, protects, and handles information when you use our mobile application across Android devices.

2. Local Sadhana & Reading Data

The core features of VedaPrashna operate entirely on-device without cloud synchronization:

  • 108 Japa Mala Counters: All bead counts, completion cycles, and target milestones are stored exclusively in your device's sandboxed local storage (AsyncStorage).
  • Verse Progress & Streaks: The 700-verse reading tracker and dawn sadhana records remain on your hardware.
  • Data Deletion: Uninstalling the application or clearing its cache from Android settings completely purges all stored sadhana metrics.

3. Saarathi AI Spiritual Counselor

VedaPrashna includes Saarathi AI, an intelligent spiritual guidance companion:

  • Your spiritual queries are transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS solely to generate responses strictly grounded in the verses of the Bhagavad Gita.
  • Queries are ephemeral and are not stored in permanent user-linked accounts.
  • We do not share your private queries with data brokers, ad networks, or commercial telemarketers.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and server infrastructure, VedaPrashna incorporates non-intrusive advertising powered by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):

  • AdMob may process pseudonymous device identifiers, network state, and coarse analytics to deliver advertisements in full compliance with Google Play Developer policies.
  • Users may view rewarded video ads at their discretion to receive supplementary AI reflection credits.
  • For details on Google's data processing, consult the Google Privacy & Terms Policy.

5. Android Device Permissions

VedaPrashna requests only permissions strictly necessary for its spiritual utility:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for streaming authentic Sanskrit verse chanting, fetching spiritual counsel, and serving AdMob advertisements.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Compliant with Android 14+ guidelines to maintain continuous acoustic Tanpura drone playback while your screen is turned off during meditation.
  • VIBRATE: Drives tactile haptic feedback for each bead counted during 108 Japa Mala chanting.

6. Children's Privacy

VedaPrashna is designed for audiences interested in philosophy and meditation. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any such data has inadvertently been processed, please contact us immediately.

7. Security of Data

All network communications between VedaPrashna and external endpoints use industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Offline data resides within Android's sandboxed private storage.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any modifications will be reflected with an updated effective date on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
