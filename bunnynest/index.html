Privacy Policy for BunnyNest

Application ID: com.bunnynest.game • Effective Date: November 18, 2026 • Last Updated: November 18, 2026
Family Safe • Local-First Gameplay • Zero Personal Tracking

Executive Summary: BunnyNest is a calming, offline-friendly spatial logic puzzle game engineered by Devang Bhumbhaliya Babubhai. All gameplay progress (500 levels, unlocked rabbit companions, high scores, customization themes, audio settings) is stored strictly on your local device. We do not operate remote databases, do not require user accounts or profiles, and do not harvest, track, or sell personal data. Advertising provided via Google AdMob is strictly configured in G-rated Family-Safe mode in full compliance with the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game BunnyNest — Cozy Spatial Logic Puzzle (referred to as "the Game", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), engineered and maintained by Devang Bhumbhaliya Babubhai ("Developer"), distributed via the Google Play Store under package identifier com.bunnynest.game.

We are deeply committed to respecting your digital privacy. This policy informs players, parents, and legal guardians of our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when playing BunnyNest.

2. Local-First Architecture & Data We Do NOT Collect

BunnyNest is built from the ground up around a sovereign, local-first paradigm:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need an email, phone number, or social media login to play the full campaign and all 500 levels.
  • No Personal Identification: We never collect names, phone numbers, contact books, biometric identifiers, GPS locations, or hardware serial numbers.
  • On-Device Progress Storage: All puzzle state (completed levels, unlocked rabbit companions, golden carrots, lucky spin status, audio toggles, and color theme preferences) is saved exclusively on your local device via sandboxed on-device application storage (localStorage / IndexedDB).
  • Offline By Default: The entire game engine, logic generator, and rabbit collections run natively on your device without requiring an active internet connection.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

BunnyNest is free to download and supported by transparent, family-safe mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To ensure full protection for players of all ages, our advertising integration strictly enforces the following standards:

  • Family-Safe & Child-Directed Configuration: The game initializes AdMob with tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true, tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true, and maxAdContentRating = General (G).
  • Non-Personalized Ads: In accordance with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA regulations, behavioral profiling, tracking cookies, and interest-based ad targeting are completely disabled. Any ads served are purely contextual and strictly suitable for all audiences.
  • Voluntary Rewarded Ads: Players may choose to watch short video advertisements voluntarily to earn bonus in-game rewards (such as extra hints or golden carrots). These rewards are completely optional and never forced.
  • Frequency Caps: Non-intrusive interstitial ads are strictly rate-limited and never interrupt active puzzle gameplay or spatial calculations.
  • Google's Privacy Policy: For more details regarding Google's ad serving and data processing practices, please consult the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Policies.

4. Android Device Permissions

BunnyNest requests only the minimum essential operating system permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Allows the game to fetch family-safe advertisements and voluntary rewarded video ads when connected to the internet.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the game to detect offline mode and gracefully disable ad requests when no network connection is available.

BunnyNest does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage files, or phone state.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

BunnyNest is designed for general audiences and families. Because our game does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data, and because all third-party advertising is locked in strict G-rated non-personalized mode, we fully comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If a parent or guardian discovers that any personal data has been transmitted inadvertently, please contact us immediately, and we will take prompt corrective action.

6. Data Security & Data Deletion

Because all gameplay data resides entirely on your device, you have complete control over your data at all times. You can permanently erase all progress, saved themes, and scores at any time by clearing the application storage via Android Settings (Settings > Apps > BunnyNest > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the application.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product enhancements, legal updates, or changes in regulatory standards. Any revisions will be published immediately on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage players to review this page periodically.
