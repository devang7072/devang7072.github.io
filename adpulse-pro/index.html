AdPulse Pro: AdMob Analytics & Insights

Privacy Policy · Effective Date: November 15, 2026
Privacy Commitment: AdPulse Pro connects directly from your device to the official Google AdMob API. We do not operate external tracking servers, do not collect personal analytics, and never store or monetize your advertising statistics.

1. Overview

AdPulse Pro ("the Application") is an Android revenue analytics suite designed for Google AdMob publishers. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent commitment to your privacy when using our application.

2. Google OAuth 2.0 & Scopes Requested

To provide real-time dashboard analytics, AdPulse Pro requests user authorization directly through official Google Play Services OAuth 2.0 protocols for the following scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.readonly: Read-only access used exclusively to list your registered applications and ad units.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admob.report: Read-only access used to query revenue reports, impressions, clicks, and eCPM statistics.
  • email and profile: Used solely to display your identity and publisher ID within the app.

3. Data Transmission & On-Device Storage

Direct Client-to-Google Communication: All network requests are dispatched securely over TLS/HTTPS directly between your mobile phone and Google's official endpoints (https://admob.googleapis.com/v1). No financial data is ever routed through or stored on third-party proxy servers.

Local Preferences: User settings (display currency, date range filters, and theme preferences) are stored strictly on-device using Android SharedPreferences.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

AdPulse Pro does not sell, lease, trade, or distribute your account information or revenue metrics to any advertisers or third-party analytical SDKs. The app contains zero advertising SDKs and zero tracking telemetry.

5. Revocation & Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your account permissions. You can disconnect your account at any time by:

  • Tapping Sign Out in the Settings screen of AdPulse Pro.
  • Visiting your Google Account Permissions and revoking access to AdPulse Pro.
  • Uninstalling the app, which instantly erases all local device state.
