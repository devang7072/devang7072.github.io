1. Overview and Description of Service

OmniScan: QR & Barcode AI is a high-speed, utility mobile application built for Android. The App provides:

  • Fast, on-device optical recognition of 1D barcodes and 2D QR codes using the device camera or local gallery images.
  • Customizable QR code generation (including Wi-Fi, URL, contact vCard, SMS, email, text, and calendar events).
  • Local scanning history and favorite records stored securely on the user's local device storage.
  • Direct smart actions (opening trusted links in browser, connecting to Wi-Fi networks, importing contacts, or copying raw scanned payloads).
