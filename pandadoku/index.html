Privacy Policy for PandaDoku

Application ID: com.devang.pandadoku • Effective Date: November 16, 2026 • Last Updated: November 16, 2026
Family Safe • Local-First Gameplay • Zero Personal Tracking

Executive Summary: PandaDoku is an offline-friendly, logic-first territory puzzle game engineered by Devang Bhumbhaliya Babubhai. All gameplay progress (500 levels, daily streak, hints, hearts, high scores) is stored exclusively on your device. We do not operate remote databases, do not require user accounts or personal profiles, and do not collect, harvest, or sell personal information. Third-party advertising provided by Google AdMob is strictly configured in G-rated Family-Safe mode in full compliance with the Google Play Families Policy.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game PandaDoku — 3D Panda Territory Logic Puzzle (referred to as "the Game", "the Application", "we", "us", or "our"), engineered by Devang Bhumbhaliya Babubhai ("Developer"), distributed via the Google Play Store under package identifier com.devang.pandadoku.

We believe sovereign privacy and pure gameplay go hand-in-hand. This policy informs players, parents, and legal guardians of our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when playing PandaDoku.

2. Local-First Architecture & Data We Do NOT Collect

PandaDoku is built around a local-first paradigm:

  • No Account Registration: You do not need to register an account, sign in via email, or link social media profiles to play the full game and all 500 campaign levels.
  • No Personal Identity Harvesting: We do not collect names, phone numbers, contact lists, GPS locations, biometric identifiers, or hardware serial numbers.
  • On-Device Progress Storage: All puzzle state (completed campaign levels, star ratings, daily challenge history, streak counters, earned hints, and player audio preferences) is stored strictly on your local device via sandboxed local storage (localStorage / on-device application storage).
  • No Cloud Game Servers: We do not operate external gameplay servers. If you play offline, the game functions completely without any internet connection.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

PandaDoku is free to play and supported by transparent, non-intrusive mobile advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To protect children and families, our AdMob integration strictly enforces the following policies:

  • Family-Safe & Child-Directed Configuration: The game programmatically initializes AdMob with tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true, tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true, and maxAdContentRating = General (G).
  • Non-Personalized Ads: In accordance with Google Play's Families Policy and COPPA, personalized or interest-based behavioral profiling is disabled. Ads shown are contextual and strictly appropriate for general audiences.
  • Non-Intrusive Banner Placement: Top-aligned adaptive banners are placed with safe-area spacing and never cover gameplay controls or tiles.
  • Voluntary Rewarded Ads: Players may choose to watch short video ads voluntarily in exchange for in-game assistance (+1 Hint, +1 Logic Scan, or +1 Heart Revive). These ads are never forced.
  • Paced Interstitial Ads: Short interstitial ads only appear between completed puzzles every 3 levels with strict frequency caps and a cooldown period. They never interrupt active puzzle solving.
  • Google's Privacy Policy: For details on how Google processes ad delivery information, please review the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Policies.

4. Android Device Permissions

PandaDoku requests only the bare minimum permissions required for game functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely to load family-safe Google AdMob advertisements and voluntary rewarded video ads when connected to the internet.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Enables the game to detect offline mode and disable ad requests gracefully when no internet connection is present.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Drives subtle, pleasant haptic feedback when tapping tiles, placing pandas, or completing levels. This can be toggled on or off at any time in the in-game Settings.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: PandaDoku does NOT request Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, SMS, Storage, or Phone State permissions.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

PandaDoku is designed for players of all ages, including children and families:

  • We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K).
  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Advertisements served are certified family-safe and rated General (G) through Google AdMob's Families-compliant ad network.
  • Parents or guardians with questions regarding their child's privacy may contact us at any time.

6. User Control & Data Deletion Rights

Because your gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device:

  • In-App Reset: You can reset all levels, unlocked content, and streak records at any time from within the game's Settings menu.
  • Complete Erasure: Clearing the app's cache/data via your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > PandaDoku > Clear Storage) or uninstalling the application instantly and permanently erases all saved gameplay data from your device.

7. Security Safeguards

PandaDoku is compiled with Google's modern Android Gradle build tools, official Capacitor native bridge, and signed with a 2048-bit RSA release key. The application enforces HTTPS security for all external resources and adheres to standard Android sandboxing security models.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be published at this permanent URL (https://devang7072.github.io/pandadoku/) with an updated "Last Updated" date.
