Privacy Policy for BabyCare Tracker

Application ID: com.babycaretracker.app • Effective Date: November 14, 2026 • Last Updated: November 14, 2026
Local-First Storage • Zero Third-Party Trackers • Child Safety

Executive Summary: BabyCare Tracker is designed to provide parents, legal guardians, and adult caregivers with a mindful, private tool for tracking infant feeding, nursing durations, sleep patterns, diaper changes, and WHO growth milestones. All sensitive infant records and health metrics are stored 100% locally on your device in an encrypted private Room/SQLite database. We do not operate cloud databases, do not collect personal infant information, do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, and do not track or sell your family's data.

1. Introduction & Applicability

This Privacy Policy describes how BabyCare Tracker ("we", "us", "our", or "the Application"), engineered by Devang Bhumbhaliya Babubhai, respects and safeguards user privacy when you use our Android application. We are committed to complete transparency and to complying with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play User Data Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Zero Remote Data Collection & Local-First Architecture

BabyCare Tracker operates on a strict local-first, privacy-by-design paradigm:

  • Local SQLite Database: All baby profiles, nursing stopwatch logs, bottle feeding quantities, sleep sessions, diaper logs, and growth measurements (weight, height, head circumference) are written directly to your device's private, sandboxed internal storage (/data/data/com.babycaretracker.app/databases/).
  • No Server Uploads: The application does not transmit or back up your child's data to external cloud servers, remote databases, or third-party web services.
  • No User Account or Registration Required: You do not need to create an account, log in with social profiles, or submit an email address to use the core application.
  • Zero Advertising or Tracking SDKs: BabyCare Tracker contains no third-party ad networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity, or AppLovin) and no third-party analytics or telemetry frameworks (such as Firebase Analytics, AppsFlyer, Facebook SDK, or Mixpanel).

3. Information Handled Strictly on Your Device

The information entered into the application remains solely under your physical custody:

  • Infant Profile Data: Baby's name, date of birth, gender, gestational status, and birth measurements.
  • Daily Care Activities: Left/right nursing stopwatch intervals, bottle formula/milk volume in ml/oz, sleep cycle start/end timestamps, diaper change details, and optional parent notes.
  • Growth Benchmarks: Weight (kg/lbs), height/length (cm/in), and head circumference evaluated locally against standard World Health Organization (WHO) reference percentiles.
  • Local Preferences: Measurement unit settings (metric vs. imperial) and interface language configurations.

4. Android Device Permissions

BabyCare Tracker adheres to the principle of least privilege and requests only the minimal permissions necessary for core utility:

  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ solely to post scheduled local alarm reminders (e.g., next feeding interval, diaper check) that you explicitly configure.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides subtle tactile haptic vibration when an alarm or timer completes.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Standard network capability used exclusively when you click external hyperlinks (such as opening this Privacy Policy in your web browser). No user data or infant records are ever transmitted over the network.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The application does not request access to Camera, Microphone, GPS Location, Contacts, Call Logs, Phone State, or External Shared Storage.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

BabyCare Tracker is designed exclusively for use by parents, legal guardians, and adult caregivers aged 18 and older. The application is not directed toward, marketed to, or intended to be operated directly by children under 13 years of age. Because the application does not collect, harvest, or transmit personal information to any server, we do not knowingly collect or process personal information from children.

6. Medical & Health Disclaimer

BabyCare Tracker is intended solely for personal developmental tracking and informational convenience. The application is not a clinical medical device and does not provide pediatric medical advice, diagnosis, or clinical treatment recommendations. The WHO growth references provided in the app are standard population benchmarks and do not replace personalized pediatric consultations. Always consult a qualified pediatrician or licensed healthcare professional regarding questions about your baby's health, feeding, or development.

7. Data Retention, Portability & Right to Erasure (GDPR)

Because all data resides exclusively on your local device, you retain total ownership and control over your records:

  • Instant Data Erasure: You can permanently, instantly, and irrevocably purge all baby profiles, activity logs, and settings at any time by tapping Settings > Wipe All Local Data inside the application.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application from your Android device causes the operating system to permanently delete the application's private storage sandbox, including all local SQLite database files.

8. Security Safeguards

We ensure that your data is handled with the highest security standards:

  • The application binary is compiled using Google's modern Android Gradle Plugin, ProGuard/R8 code optimization, and Google Play App Signing.
  • The application does not use unencrypted cleartext network traffic (cleartextTrafficPermitted="false" by default).
  • All database operations utilize Room's parameterized queries, preventing SQL injection vulnerabilities.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates, legal obligations, or Google Play policy updates. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
