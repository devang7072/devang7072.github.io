Core Privacy Commitment: Kika Status Downloader operates under an offline-first, on-device architecture. All your saved status photos, videos, audio voice notes, and notification-recovered messages remain strictly on your local device storage. We do not operate external cloud servers, nor do we track, collect, sell, or transmit your media.

1. Information We Process

Kika Status Downloader is designed to respect your digital autonomy. The application processes:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account, log in, or provide personal credentials (name, email, phone number, address) to use the app.
  • Status Media (Photos, Videos, Audio): Status files that you have already viewed in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business are read locally via Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) and saved directly into your device's standard MediaStore / Gallery only when you explicitly tap the download button.
  • Deleted Message Backups: If you voluntarily enable the notification listener, incoming WhatsApp message alerts are parsed locally on your device into an encrypted SQLite (Room) database so you can read messages that contacts later delete. This data never leaves your device.
  • Video Story Slices: The 30-second video splitter runs entirely through Android hardware MediaExtractor and MediaMuxer on your local chipset. No video is ever uploaded to external servers.

2. Android Permissions & Hardware Justification

In strict alignment with Google Play Developer Policy and Android 15 (Target SDK 35) Scoped Storage rules, Kika Status Downloader requests only essential permissions:

  • Storage Access Framework (SAF Tree URI): We prompt you to select and grant read-only folder access to the standard Android/media/com.whatsapp folder. This allows the app to display viewed statuses without requesting broad, dangerous legacy storage permissions.
  • Notification Listener Access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE): Required solely for the optional message recovery feature. It allows the app to detect incoming text notifications and recognize revocation events ("This message was deleted").
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used on Android 13+ to notify you when status downloads or video splitting operations finish.
  • Network Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used exclusively to deliver Google AdMob advertisements and render the desktop WhatsApp Web portal if you link a secondary account.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Kika Status Downloader displays non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-7686687473347025) to support continuous engineering updates and maintenance.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and general diagnostic logs to serve fraud-free, compliant advertisements. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

For more details on Google's advertising data practices, please consult the Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Kika Status Downloader is a general mobile utility application intended for audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

Because all status media and recovered chats are stored locally on your device:

  • You maintain complete ownership and control over your files at all times.
  • You can delete individual downloaded statuses directly from the "Saved" tab or your phone's Gallery.
  • You can delete message recovery logs from the "Recovery" tab.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing app storage in Android System Settings permanently purges all local app database records.
