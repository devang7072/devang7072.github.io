Privacy Policy for AirFlow AC Remote

Application: AirFlow AC Remote (com.climaair.app.airflow_ac_remote)

Effective Date: November 6, 2026

Developer: Devang Bhumbhaliya Babubhai

Summary: AirFlow AC Remote operates with an offline-first privacy architecture. All your paired air conditioners, room configurations, and temperature settings are stored strictly on your local device. We do not transmit, sell, or collect your personal data on external servers.

1. Information We Process

AirFlow AC Remote transforms your smartphone into a universal infrared air conditioner remote control. In doing so, the application processes:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect your name, phone number, contacts, exact GPS location, photos, camera, or microphone access.
  • AC Configuration & Profiles: The remote models you test, custom room labels (e.g., Living Room, Master Bedroom), and preferred temperature/fan modes are stored locally on your device via Android private app storage (SharedPreferences).

2. Device Hardware & Permissions

AirFlow AC Remote requests only the minimum hardware permissions necessary to operate an appliance remote control:

  • android.permission.TRANSMIT_IR: Used strictly to emit consumer infrared carrier pulses from your phone's built-in IR blaster diode to air conditioning units. No transmission history or usage signals are logged or uploaded.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback pulses when rotating the rotary temperature knob or tapping remote control buttons.
  • android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used solely by Google AdMob to load advertisements. The core remote control functionality remains 100% operational offline without an active internet connection.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

The application displays banner, interstitial, and app open advertisements powered by Google AdMob (Publisher ID: pub-7686687473347025) to support ongoing engineering and brand database updates. Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and crash logs to serve compliant, fraud-free ad experiences.

For additional details regarding Google's data handling practices, please refer to the Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

4. Children's Privacy

AirFlow AC Remote is a general utility designed for controlling household air conditioning appliances. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Security of Your Data

Because your remote profiles and temperature settings never leave your device, they remain insulated from external data breaches. You can delete all application data at any time by clearing the application storage via Android system settings or uninstalling the app.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements, new AC protocol dictionaries, or regulatory requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
